metadata in `CITATION.cff` use the matching SPDX identifier `MIT`.

tests, checked-in thesis figures and tables, and project-generated frozen research

evidence, its provenance, or the thesis conclusions.

Raw datasets and other third-party materials are not relicensed by DANIDS. The raw
datasets are not distributed in the Git source release and must be obtained separately
under their providers' terms.

Citation and licensing are separate: the MIT License permits reuse under its terms, and
users of the software or frozen research artifacts should also follow the citation
metadata in [`CITATION.cff`](../CITATION.cff).
